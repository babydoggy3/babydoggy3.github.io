## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Owners and managers of small, owner-run businesses in Bohol, Philippines (Tagbilaran City, Panglao, nearby towns): cafes and restaurants, resorts, clinics (vet, dental, beauty), shops/pasalubong, schools. They usually arrive from a Facebook/Messenger message or a link the owner sends, on a phone, often between customers. Job: decide in a minute whether this person can make their business look good online and is worth a message.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Claude Daigan's freelance social media & marketing service. Success = the visitor sends a message (Viber/call or email) asking for the free online check-up, a free sample, or a package.

## Positioning

A local, one-person service in Tagbilaran that makes reels, designed posts and Google Business Profile work from the business's OWN photos, with fixed monthly packages and a free check-up/sample before any payment. Not an agency with stock-photo templates; not a Manila/overseas agency.

## Operating Context

- Lead-generation flow (from the owner's Services sheet): Free Online Check-up (1-page review, 2 days) or Free Sample Pack (1 reel + 3 posts from their own photos, 2–3 days) → Starter Setup → monthly plan.
- The owner also works a night shift; replies within 1 business day, Mon–Sat.
- Facebook Page "Claude Daigan – Social Media & Marketing" is being set up but is not live yet.

- Free Sample Pack: ₱0, 1 reel + 3 post designs using the business's own photos, 2–3 days, limited to 2–3 per month.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work first; the samples are the pitch.
2. Prices and process are plain and upfront; the free check-up lowers the first step.
3. Local and personal: a real person in Tagbilaran, reachable on Viber.
4. Truthful: concept work is labelled, nothing is promised that the terms exclude.
